# Artifact Guide

Each artifact exists to carry authority, intent, constraints, evidence, or learning across a lifecycle boundary.

Every artifact should state:

- purpose;
- status and version;
- accountable human owner;
- AI contribution, if any;
- authority sources;
- decisions and unresolved questions;
- related chapter;
- revision history.

## Status language

- **Draft:** incomplete and not authoritative.
- **Reviewed:** examined by the named role but not accepted.
- **Accepted:** approved by the accountable human owner for its stated purpose.
- **Superseded:** retained for history but no longer authoritative.

AI may draft, analyze, transform, and review. AI does not assign final acceptance to its own contribution.

## Evidence language

Use passed, failed, blocked, and not run. Preserve environmental failures separately from assertion failures. A successful rerun should reference the earlier condition rather than erase it.
