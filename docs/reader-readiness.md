# Reader Readiness and Publication Gate

## Question

Can a reader complete the PetClinic practice track and transfer the method to another software project without asking the author for clarification?

## Current answer

The repository now provides the necessary path: pinned baseline, setup instructions, cumulative chapter map, context, requirements, architecture, decisions, work packages, contribution contracts, structured AI instructions, implementation, tests, review evidence, security and governance decisions, release controls, operating guidance, adoption guidance, and a reader-project transfer exercise.

That makes the path complete on paper and executable on the verified environment. It does not yet prove that an independent reader can follow it without clarification.

## Publication gates

Before claiming reader self-sufficiency:

1. Give the book and a clean copy of this repository to at least one target reader who did not help create them.
2. Do not coach the reader during the exercise.
3. Record every question, ambiguous instruction, failed command, environment assumption, and missing artifact.
4. Correct material defects in the book or companion.
5. Ask the reader to apply the same method to one small change in their own software.
6. Verify that the reader can explain the role of each artifact, the human decision boundary, and the evidence required for acceptance.

The controlled experiment must also be executed under both documented conditions before the book presents comparative observations.

## Current disposition

**Technical reference implementation:** Verified for the documented local scope.  
**Reader self-sufficiency:** Not yet independently validated.  
**Publication package:** Not yet publication-ready.

The remaining work is validation, not another redesign of the methodology.
