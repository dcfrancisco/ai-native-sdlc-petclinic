# Evidence Provenance

The companion distinguishes evidence from illustration.

| Label | Meaning | Acceptance use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Established Engineering Evidence | Supported by cited standards, research, or established engineering literature | Supports general engineering claims within the source's limits |
| Verified Reference Implementation | Implemented and checked in this repository with recorded commands and limitations | Supports claims about the bounded PetClinic capability |
| Recorded Engineering Journey | A decision, contribution, failure, correction, or limitation supported by project artifacts | Supports the recorded engineering event whether or not the complete product is finished; does not establish unobserved production or business outcomes |
| Educational Replay | Re-executed later under recorded conditions to reconstruct a learning sequence | Supports only the recorded replay |
| Illustrative Example | Written to explain a concept and not executed | Cannot support an acceptance claim |

## Rules

- Do not convert an illustrative example into evidence by removing its label.
- Do not call local-adapter acceptance provider delivery or owner receipt.
- Record failed and not-run checks.
- Identify the source revision, date, tools, commands, and limitations for verified evidence.
- The author's product journeys belong in the book when they teach the
  engineering decision. The PetClinic companion remains the reproducible reader
  laboratory and need not contain copies of those product repositories.
- Project completion is not an evidence requirement. Match each claim to the
  decision, correction, test, review, or operational result that was actually
  recorded.
- Human acceptance remains a decision informed by evidence, not a property generated by the AI contributor.
