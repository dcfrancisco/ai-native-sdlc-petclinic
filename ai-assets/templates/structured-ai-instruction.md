# Structured AI Instruction Template

**Book chapter:** 8

## Role

State one primary responsibility.

## Objective

State the bounded outcome.

## Authority

List authoritative artifacts and decision owners.

## Context and current state

Describe implemented behavior, relevant history, and known uncertainty.

## Scope

List permitted work.

## Exclusions

List work that must not be performed.

## Constraints and invariants

State properties that must remain true.

## Required deliverables

List artifacts and evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

Reference observable outcomes owned by the human acceptance process.

## Verification

Specify focused, regression, integration, and evidence reporting.

## Functional checks for the reader

Assign behavior requiring human observation.

## Docker or environment checks

Specify relevant environment checks and cleanup.

## Reporting requirements

Require concise results, limitations, and modified files.

## Stop conditions

State when to stop and escalate.
